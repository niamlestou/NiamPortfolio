Context

The cast and the method

It began as a paper-trading bot for SPY and QQQ and became a research program asking one question: can a small local model add predictive power that a plain regression cannot?

Every attempt was held to the same method. A hypothesis and a variant budget are written down first. Data is split into train, validation and test by date. Any new data goes to a ridge logistic before a model sees it (bot/datagate.py), and a result counts only if its 95% day-block interval excludes zero.

The local models

  • Laya: a ModernBERT encoder with a typed head, served locally by laya-serve. Checkpoint typed-decisions is pinned; fine-tunes are ft1, ft2a/b/c, laya-tb, stocks_ft and fx_ft.
  • Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507: a 4-bit model run through Ollama on a 4 GB GPU. It fills fixed fields per headline (battery tx1).
  • Qwen fine-tunes d1 and d2: adapters trained on headline direction.

Not local, and why it is here

  • TypeSafe's Jev gated the first replay. It was removed when the project committed to Laya, so reports call it "the earlier model".
  • The ridge logistic is not a local AI model, but it is the yardstick every model had to beat.

Judgment

Rules and risks

The kill rules below come from the pre-registrations. Read them before changing a threshold.

Ifthe V3 validation book is not above zero after 20 bps, or does not beat the equal-weight universe.

ThenH5 ends as "no text edge found". The next idea is a new pre-registration.

Ifany scored day falls on or before a model's cutoff.

Thenthe run is void.

Ifthe book is positive gross but not net.

Thenlengthen the hold once; never lower the cost.

Ifthe forward test is below -200 bps cumulative net excess after day 60.

Thenabandon it early. Do not declare a win early either.

Answer

What should happen next?

Run the one remaining gate on the mid-cap Qwen lead, and treat every other local-model role as closed until forward days exist.

Local models have not beaten a regression on numbers anywhere, so the open question is text on single stocks over days. If the V3 book test fails, H5 ends and the project's honest result is that no local model added tradable information. If it passes, the next evidence is the Qwen2.5 check, one test look, and 120 forward days.
